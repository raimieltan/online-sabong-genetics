# UNBREAKABLE

## Behavioral Prompt

When **Unbreakable** activates, the rooster enters a state of extreme resilience and refusal to collapse.

This awakening is not about becoming faster or dramatically stronger. It is about continuing to fight through punishment that would normally break another fighter.

Behavior should change immediately:

* posture becomes heavier and more grounded
* stance widens slightly
* feet plant firmly into the ground
* the rooster stops retreating as easily
* knockback reactions become shorter and more controlled
* pain reactions still happen, but recovery is much faster
* the rooster absorbs hits, regains balance, and keeps moving forward
* it becomes increasingly difficult to stagger or knock down
* defensive choices become more disciplined
* the rooster protects vulnerable areas instead of panicking
* it willingly accepts minor damage when doing so creates a better position
* it maintains pressure despite fatigue or injury
* movements become deliberate rather than frantic
* morale and confidence visually appear restored
* it should feel like the fighter has decided that falling is simply no longer an option

When heavily damaged, the rooster should become even more stubborn.

A heavy hit may make it stumble backward, but instead of collapsing, it catches itself, digs its claws into the ground, raises its head, and immediately returns to stance.

The emotional feeling should be:

**“You can hurt me, but you cannot stop me.”**

## Visual Prompt

Create a powerful resilience-focused awakening.

When Unbreakable activates:

* briefly slow the moment after the rooster receives a major hit
* show its feet sliding backward through dust
* claws dig into the ground and stop the movement
* small stones, dirt, or feathers scatter around its feet
* the rooster slowly raises its head again
* subtle body tremors show that it is injured but still standing
* aura should be dense and grounded rather than explosive
* use subtle pulsing energy close to the body
* energy should appear heavy, stable, and compressed
* emphasize chest breathing and physical strain
* damaged feathers should move in the energy pressure
* briefly highlight scars, injuries, dust, or battle wear
* reduce large knockback animation while awakened
* strong hits may create impact ripples around the rooster, but the body barely moves

The visual message should be:

**immovable, exhausted, damaged, but still standing.**

Avoid flashy speed effects.

This awakening should look like sheer willpower becoming physically visible.

# BERSERKER

## Behavioral Prompt

When **Berserker** activates, the rooster abandons normal caution and enters an overwhelming aggression state.

This is controlled only by instinct and violence.

Behavior changes dramatically:

* aggression rises to maximum
* attack frequency increases
* hesitation disappears
* the rooster constantly attempts to close distance
* it becomes willing to trade damage
* defensive patience drops
* attacks become heavier and more committed
* combos become longer
* recovery windows become more dangerous because the rooster immediately tries to attack again
* it pursues retreating opponents aggressively
* it frequently interrupts or crashes into enemy attacks
* it uses wings, body weight, kicks, pecks, and charges more violently
* movement becomes explosive and unpredictable
* idle circling becomes stalking and forward pressure
* the rooster may attack from awkward positions it would normally avoid
* pain can temporarily increase aggression rather than causing retreat
* taking damage can trigger immediate retaliation
* the rooster becomes less concerned with perfect positioning and more concerned with overwhelming the opponent

The rooster should look furious but still animalistically intelligent.

It should not randomly flail.

Its instinct should become:

**close distance → overwhelm → keep attacking → never allow recovery.**

The emotional message should be:

**“If you hit me, I hit you harder.”**

## Visual Prompt

Create an explosive rage awakening.

At activation:

* rooster violently throws its head upward
* feathers flare outward
* wings partially spread
* claws scrape against the ground
* strong pressure wave bursts outward
* dust and loose feathers are blown away
* aura violently erupts around the rooster
* energy should flicker unpredictably rather than flow smoothly
* use aggressive particle bursts
* slight camera shake on heavy movements
* stronger hit-stop on Berserker attacks
* motion blur increases during lunges
* rapid wing and feather movement emphasizes unstable energy
* eyes become intensely focused
* heavy breathing becomes visible
* attacks create stronger air trails
* footsteps and landings kick up more debris

The aura should constantly surge and collapse around the body like unstable pressure.

During attacks, briefly intensify the aura.

When damaged, the aura may spike instead of weakening.

The overall visual feeling should be:

**violent momentum, overwhelming pressure, barely contained aggression.**

# FLOW STATE

## Behavioral Prompt

When **Flow State** activates, the rooster enters perfect instinctive combat awareness.

This is not rage.

This is not brute strength.

The rooster becomes extremely calm.

Behavior changes:

* unnecessary movement disappears
* posture becomes relaxed but perfectly balanced
* panic reactions are suppressed
* the rooster waits for genuine openings
* reaction timing becomes exceptionally precise
* tells are recognized earlier
* attacks are avoided using minimal movement
* head slips, pivots, weight shifts, short sidesteps, and subtle body rotations replace large dodges
* the rooster stops overcommitting
* counters happen immediately after successful reads
* spacing becomes extremely efficient
* it naturally moves toward superior angles
* recovery discipline improves
* it avoids unnecessary trades
* aggression becomes situational instead of emotional
* the rooster smoothly transitions between evade, reposition, counter, pressure, and disengage
* opponent overextensions are punished immediately
* movement feels automatic rather than consciously chosen

The rooster should appear to understand attacks before they fully happen.

Its body reacts almost independently of thought.

The behavior loop becomes:

**observe → read → evade → reposition → punish → return to calm.**

Perfect reads should create especially dramatic evasions.

The emotional message is:

**“The attack has already been understood before it arrives.”**

## Visual Prompt

Create an awakening based on perfect instinct, perception, and effortless movement.

At activation:

* surrounding motion briefly slows
* the rooster becomes extremely still
* breathing becomes controlled
* head movement becomes subtle
* eyes lock onto the opponent
* ambient sound and visual noise feel reduced
* a restrained flowing aura forms close to the body
* use smooth, elegant energy rather than explosive flames
* subtle air distortion surrounds the rooster
* loose feathers may slowly float around it
* movement becomes unusually crisp compared with the environment

During evasive actions, create a **temporal afterimage effect**.

The rooster should appear in multiple moments of its movement simultaneously.

When dodging:

* the real rooster smoothly leaves the attack path
* 2–4 translucent previous poses remain visible behind it
* each afterimage should represent a real previous animation pose
* afterimages should follow the exact motion trajectory
* older images become progressively more transparent
* they fade within a fraction of a second
* the real rooster remains the sharpest and most solid
* wings, tail, feet, and head may have slight motion trails
* attacks can briefly appear to pass through the old-position ghost
* the real rooster may already be standing beside the attacker when the afterimage fades

Do not make this look like teleportation or clones.

It should feel like several moments of extremely fast movement have become visible at once.

Perfect-read sequence:

**OPPONENT COMMITS → WORLD SLOWS → ROOSTER SLIPS ATTACK → AFTERIMAGE REMAINS WHERE IT WAS → REAL ROOSTER APPEARS AT A SUPERIOR ANGLE → IMMEDIATE COUNTER → AFTERIMAGE DISSOLVES.**

The strongest afterimage effects should appear only during perfect reads, clutch evasions, and major Flow-State moments.

Overall feeling:

**effortless, untouchable, precise, calm, instinctive.**

# SECOND WIND

## Behavioral Prompt

When **Second Wind** activates, the rooster suddenly recovers fighting capacity after appearing exhausted.

Unlike Unbreakable, which ignores suffering, Second Wind feels like the body suddenly discovers another reserve of energy.

Before activation, the rooster should visibly look tired:

* slower movement
* heavy breathing
* lowered wings
* reduced aggression
* slower recovery
* occasional hesitation

Then the awakening happens.

Behavior changes:

* breathing stabilizes
* movement speed returns
* posture rises
* reaction quality improves
* footwork becomes lighter
* stamina-consuming actions become possible again
* attack commitment increases
* defensive movement becomes sharper
* the rooster becomes willing to pressure again
* recovery between exchanges becomes faster
* confidence visibly returns
* previously sluggish movements become crisp
* the fighter suddenly feels dangerous again

The important contrast is:

**exhausted fighter → sudden recovery → renewed competitive threat.**

Second Wind should not make the rooster supernaturally perfect.

It should feel like a dramatic restoration of physical capability.

The emotional message:

**“I am not finished yet.”**

## Visual Prompt

Create a rejuvenation awakening.

Before activation:

* rooster breathes heavily
* chest movement is exaggerated
* posture is lowered
* wings hang slightly
* movements feel heavy

Activation:

* rooster takes one deep breath
* brief silence or visual stillness
* chest expands
* head lifts
* feathers subtly rise
* energy begins flowing from the torso outward
* a soft pulse travels through the body
* legs visibly regain stability
* posture becomes upright
* eyes regain focus
* dust or loose feathers gently push outward

Instead of an explosive aura, use a revitalizing pulse.

Energy may briefly flow along the body like circulation returning.

After activation:

* animation timing becomes sharper
* footwork becomes lighter
* recovery animations shorten
* heavy breathing reduces
* subtle energy trails appear during renewed movement

The visual effect should communicate:

**energy returning to a body that had almost emptied itself.**

Keep it hopeful, dramatic, and physically believable rather than godlike.

# APEX

## Behavioral Prompt

When **Apex** activates, the rooster enters its maximum combat output state.

Apex represents the fighter temporarily exceeding its normal physical limits.

Unlike Flow State, which emphasizes perfect instinct, Apex emphasizes:

**POWER + SPEED + PRESENCE + CONFIDENCE.**

Behavior changes dramatically:

* posture becomes dominant
* confidence rises sharply
* hesitation disappears
* attacks gain much stronger commitment
* acceleration increases
* movement becomes more explosive
* pressure increases
* the rooster actively takes control of the center
* opponents are forced backward more often
* heavy attacks become more frequent
* combos become faster
* recovery becomes more aggressive
* attacks transition immediately into follow-up pressure
* jumps and lunges cover greater distance
* defensive reactions remain competent but become secondary to domination
* successful attacks cause the rooster to push harder
* the rooster behaves as though it knows it is currently stronger than its opponent
* it challenges the opponent directly instead of waiting

Unlike Berserker, Apex remains disciplined.

It does not lose control.

Every movement should communicate overwhelming confidence.

The behavior should feel like:

**close distance → overpower → dominate position → attack again → maintain control.**

The emotional message:

**“This is my maximum.”**

## Visual Prompt

Create a dramatic full-power transformation.

This should be the most visually explosive awakening.

Activation sequence:

* rooster freezes for a brief moment
* body lowers slightly as power builds
* feathers begin vibrating
* loose debris around the feet starts moving
* energy gathers around the body
* ground particles begin lifting
* aura rapidly intensifies
* rooster raises its head or spreads its wings
* massive energy burst expands outward
* strong pressure wave pushes dust, feathers, and particles away
* camera briefly shakes
* aura stabilizes around the transformed fighter

During Apex:

* large bright aura surrounds the body
* energy rises vertically and continuously
* feathers constantly react to the energy pressure
* subtle energy sparks or arcs occasionally appear
* footsteps create stronger dust displacement
* acceleration creates pronounced motion trails
* powerful attacks generate visible air-pressure effects
* heavy impacts create stronger shockwaves
* jumps and charges create energy trails
* eyes become intensely illuminated or visually emphasized
* the rooster appears physically more imposing even without changing actual model scale

The aura should breathe with the rooster:

CALM → moderate intensity
MOVING → stronger
ATTACKING → flare
HEAVY ATTACK → massive flare
TAKING DAMAGE → temporary distortion
RECOVERING → stabilizes

Unlike Berserker's chaotic aura, Apex energy should feel **powerful but controlled**.

The fighter should look like an enormous amount of energy is being contained within a disciplined body.

For major attacks:

* aura compresses inward immediately before movement
* extremely brief pause
* explosive release
* strong acceleration blur
* trailing energy
* impact shockwave

The overall visual language should communicate:

**raw power elevated beyond the rooster's normal limits.**

Apex should feel like the ultimate physical transformation:

**dominant, explosive, overwhelming, controlled, and unmistakably powerful.**

# AWAKENING IDENTITY SUMMARY

Each awakening should remain immediately recognizable even if the UI name is hidden.

**UNBREAKABLE**
Behavior: refuses to fall.
Visual: grounded, heavy, battle-damaged resilience.

**BERSERKER**
Behavior: relentless offensive aggression.
Visual: violent, unstable, explosive energy.

**FLOW STATE**
Behavior: perfect instinct, reads, evasion, counters.
Visual: calm aura, slowed perception, temporal afterimages.

**SECOND WIND**
Behavior: exhausted fighter suddenly regains energy.
Visual: restorative pulse, posture recovery, renewed movement.

**APEX**
Behavior: maximum controlled physical output.
Visual: massive aura, explosive acceleration, overwhelming presence.
